Tokenwell well mark: ASCII launch animation frames

Each frame is 11 rows. Rows 0-5 are the sky above the well, rows 6-10 are the well
(6 rim top, 7 rim, 8 and 9 water, 10 bottom). Rows are shown exactly as drawn; trailing
spaces are not significant. Colours: coin characters gold #F2A93B, everything else
water #3FB6C0. Plain ASCII only, no backslashes in any frame.
Frame 13 is the static logo (the resting pose), the same block as LOGO at the end.
Every block under a --- line is exactly 11 lines, blank sky rows included.

=== Gold spans (coin characters) per frame
Format: row R cols S-E (0-based, end exclusive): text. Every character in the span is
gold, spaces inside a span stay blank. Characters outside the spans use the water colour.
Gold applies only to the coin: in frame 3 only '(o)' is gold, not the walls or waves; in
frame 4 only the "'-'" in the rim top is gold, never the rim underscores.
Sparks rows that carry no coin glyph (for example frame 9 rows 0 and 2) stay water,
as in the approved reference.
